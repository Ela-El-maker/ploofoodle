Common issues

  • Login/session code errors after long inactivity.
  • Delayed payment confirmations from mobile network provider.
  • Outdated app version requiring store update.

Report a problem

When contacting support, include:

  • Your app version (from profile/settings)
  • A screenshot or short screen recording
  • Exact steps that caused the issue
  • Timestamp and payment reference (for M-PESA related issues)
